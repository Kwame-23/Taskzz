# 📋 Lista Vista

**Lista Vista** is a comprehensive and user-centric application designed to facilitate the creation, management, and sharing of lists. The platform enables individuals and teams to organize tasks, ideas, and priorities efficiently, fostering productivity and collaboration.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Create and Manage Lists**  
  Develop multiple lists tailored to diverse needs, including personal tasks, project planning, and shared activities.

- **Item Management**  
  Add, edit, and remove items within lists with ease to ensure information remains current and relevant.

- **Collaboration and Sharing**  
  Share lists securely with colleagues, friends, or family members via unique links or direct invitations.

- **Customizable Interface**  
  Personalize the appearance of the application with a selection of themes and color schemes.

- **Reminders and Notifications** *(optional)*  
  Enable notifications to receive timely reminders about upcoming tasks or deadlines.
